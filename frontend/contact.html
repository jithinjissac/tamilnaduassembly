Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days (Monday-Saturday). For urgent issues, please mention "URGENT" in your subject line.

What information should I include for technical support?

Please include your Order ID, a detailed description of the issue, browser/device information, and any error messages you received. Screenshots are also helpful.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

Please refer to our Cancellation and Refunds Policy for detailed information about our refund process.

Do you offer phone support?

Phone support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM - 6 PM IST. For after-hours support, please send us an email and we'll respond as soon as possible.

How can I report a data accuracy issue?

Select "Data Accuracy" as the subject and include your Order ID and specific details about the inaccurate data. Please note that we extract data directly from the Kerala SEC portal.
